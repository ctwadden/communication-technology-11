Brand Launch Tile

Date ____________________

Choose one fictional client. Build a mini style tile and a 1080 x 1080 launch graphic. An A4 adaptation and original symbol are extension pathways.

Assignment duration: three 70-minute classes. Two-class option: combine the final test, revision and submission audit with Class B.

Three-class sprint

Class A · Decode and directChoose a client, identify audience and message, repair the weak-flyer principles, then complete the palette and type hierarchy on the style tile.
Class B · Build the messageCreate the 1080 × 1080 launch graphic with an editable image, clear hierarchy, restrained mark and consistent brand system.
Class C · Test and deliverRun a silent five-second audience test, revise the clearest mismatch, confirm sRGB/size and submit the rationale plus process evidence.

Required evidence

  1. Layered PSD with named type, image, colour and mark layers
  2. Mini style tile: four-colour palette, type pair and simple wordmark
  3. 1080 x 1080 PNG
  4. One progress screenshot and source log
  5. Three-sentence design rationale

Foundation checkpoints

Day 1A message needs an orderA partner identifies the headline, key detail and action in the intended order within five seconds.
Day 2Audience becomes colourEach colour has a job; body text meets the contrast target; the palette can be explained using the audience and brief adjectives.
Day 3Typography gives the brand a voiceA peer can identify the client and read the required line without explanation; the style tile shows repeatable Headline and Detail roles.
Day 4Images and words share one systemThe message remains readable at phone size; image, type and mark have separate editable roles; no essential text touches the edge.
Day 5Test, revise and deliverThe intended message and mood appear in at least two peer responses; all required files open; the PSD remains layered.
