# Communication Technology 11 — Graphic design review

8 October 2026 · Teacher-review draft

This package teaches design elements/principles, a labelled colour wheel, palette roles, typography/spacing and digital output for Nova Scotia Communications Technology 11 graphic design outcomes 4.1–4.5. It develops the existing local teaching package; it does not reschedule the course.

## Download and review

[Download the complete student package](COM11_GraphicDesign_Student_Package_2026-10-08_v4_DRAFT.zip), extract it, and open `index.html`. The 70-file archive contains:

- 45-slide browser presentation with explanations, ten teaching checkpoints and later connection/transfer checks;
- `downloads/COM11_GraphicDesign_2026-10-08_v3_DRAFT.pptx`: editable text and narration notes, with embedded concept/ad visuals;
- matching 45-page presentation PDF;
- twelve Photoshop practice labs, layered PSD starters, font licences and 13-page printable lab sheets;
- seven proposed 70-minute classes, reader tests/revisions and an independent approved school/community-message brief;
- Google AI Studio prompt for three short prediction/change/explanation activities. This is a specification, not an already-built simulator.

Actual campaign examples include McDonald's Follow the Arches, Coca-Cola Recycle Me, Public Theater typography and a LEGO advertising illustration. They are credited teaching/discussion examples. Constructed lab posters are labelled practice artwork. Images do not prove audience effects or environmental impacts; copyright remains with the respective owners. See the archive's `sources.html`.
