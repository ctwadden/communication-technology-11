About the examples, sources and privacy

Museum and design references ground the terminology. All geometric demonstrations, comparison layouts and studio prompts are original Learning Studio teaching examples. The school-event posters are not actual announcements. Vocabulary groupings vary across courses; this is an introduction, not an official IB rubric or a substitute for your teacher’s outcome map. The optional LEGO artwork is genuine, separately credited source material, not a simulated company campaign.

Only your last slide number is stored in this browser, when local storage is available. Quiz answers are not stored or sent to a teacher. Source images and links connect to their host websites. Browser read-aloud uses your device’s available voice services; voice availability and processing depend on the browser and operating system. Do not enter student names or personal information.

Voice, speed and playback settings

Guided playback stops at task screens. With voice off or unavailable, sentence captions advance on a reading timer. This is a browser lesson, not a recorded video. Keyboard: arrow keys for slides; space to start or stop playback.
