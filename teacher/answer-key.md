# Brand Foundations — answer key

## Knowledge check

1. **Hierarchy** — Hierarchy creates the reading order.
2. **To show related information** — Items placed near one another appear related.
3. **It fits the audience, job and legibility needs** — Colour choices need an audience and communication reason.
4. **1080 sRGB PNG** — The PNG is the web export; the PSD preserves production.
5. **The first message and hierarchy received** — It checks what the audience notices before an explanation changes their response.

## Transfer and defence prompts

1. **What makes a brand system different from one attractive poster?**
   - Listen for: A system repeats intentional type, colour, imagery, mark and spacing roles across different formats.
2. **Why diagnose the client and audience before opening Photoshop?**
   - Listen for: The audience and communication job determine what should be noticed, felt and acted on.
3. **Why create type roles rather than choosing fonts one line at a time?**
   - Listen for: Roles create consistent hierarchy and make the system transferable.
4. **Why use Place Embedded and Smart Objects?**
   - Listen for: They preserve source quality and allow reversible scaling and replacement.
5. **What does the five-second test measure?**
   - Listen for: The first message, mood and focal element actually received by a viewer.
6. **Why must an A4 adaptation be recomposed instead of stretched?**
   - Listen for: The format changes spatial relationships while the brand system should remain recognizable.
7. **What belongs in the source log?**
   - Listen for: The original creator/provider or teacher-supplied filename, license/context and how it was used.
8. **What must the defence connect?**
   - Listen for: A design decision, audience evidence, the revision it caused and a live editable layer.

Accept alternate wording when the student uses accurate evidence and can demonstrate the decision.
