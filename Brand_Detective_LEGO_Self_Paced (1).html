About the examples, sources and privacy

The logo and identity artwork are actual LEGO source assets, credited to the LEGO Group and, for the identity project, Our LEGO Agency and Interbrand. Identity artwork is reproduced by BrickNerd; the logo is served through Wikimedia Commons. Written design intentions are sourced to LEGO and Interbrand. Classroom diagrams and prompts are original teaching additions, not official campaigns or brand specifications. LEGO marks and artwork belong to their respective owners. This lesson is independent and not sponsored or endorsed by LEGO. The supplied workbook has missing numbered video questions and Step 5 instructions; supplementary video prompts here are explicitly additional practice.

Only your last slide number is stored in this browser, when local storage is available. Quiz answers are not stored or sent to a teacher. Source images and links connect to their host websites. Browser read-aloud uses your device’s available voice services; voice availability and processing depend on the browser and operating system. Do not enter student names or personal information.
