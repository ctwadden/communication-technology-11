Success criteria · Sprint 3 photography

These describe strong work, not a mark scheme. The approved rubric will be shared when confirmed.

  • I handle the camera and tripod safely every time.
  • I change a camera setting on purpose and can explain its effect.
  • My documentation photos are sharp, evenly lit, straight on, and include a scale.
  • My exports use sizes and formats that suit web and print, and I can explain them.
  • On my own, I solved a new capture problem and explained why my photo documents rather than just decorates.
  • All my evidence photos are my own originals.
