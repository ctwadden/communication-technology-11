How it actually works

A layout controls attention by creating differences and relationships. Hierarchy establishes order; alignment and proximity show which elements belong together.

CT11 · Demonstration

Demonstration · first moves

1

Read the message before styling

Weak flyer copy sheet

Headline, essential detail and call to action are marked
2

Choose one alignment system

Photoshop guides or a paper thumbnail grid

Most edges share a consistent left, centre or grid line
3

Create visible hierarchy

Type size, weight, placement and contrast

The headline reads first, essential detail second and action third
Brand Foundations
Speaker notes

Model slowly. After each move, show the expected result and the common mistake: Starting with colours and effects; Centred type plus random left-aligned details; Making everything large and bold.

CT11 · Evidence

Checkpoint and exit

Checkpoint

Exit question

Brand Foundations
Speaker notes
