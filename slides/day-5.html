CT11 · Evidence

Checkpoint and exit

Checkpoint

The intended message and mood appear in at least two peer responses; all required files open; the PSD remains layered.

Exit question

Which audience response confirmed or challenged your design intention?

Brand Foundations
Speaker notes

Inspect the evidence rather than accepting a completion check. Use the exit response to decide tomorrow's two-minute retrieval prompt.
